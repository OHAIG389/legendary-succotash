Charlton Heston

10/4/1923-4/5/2008 (94)
Actor; Played Astronaut George Taylor

From Wikipedia
"Charlton Heston (born John Charles Carter) was an American actor and political activist. He gained stardom for his leading man roles in numerous Hollywood films including biblical epics, historical drama films, science-fiction films, and action films ... In the 1950s and early 1960s, he was one of a handful of Hollywood actors who openly denounced racism and he was also an active supporter of the civil rights movement ... [After Heston's death] President George W. Bush called Heston 'a man of character and integrity, with a big heart ... He served his country during World War II, marched in the civil rights movement, led a labor union and vigorously defended Americans' Second Amendment rights.'"

Roddy McDowall

9/17/1928-10/3/1998 (70)
Actor; Played Doctor Cornelius; Caesar

From Wikipedia
"Roderick Andrew Anthony Jude McDowall was a British and American actor with a career spanning six decades on stage and screen ... He subsequently appeared in a variety of film and television roles, notably as Cornelius and Caesar in the Planet of the Apes films (1968–73), and ... was a founding member of the National Film Preservation Board and represented the Screen Actors Guild on that board until his death. He was also active as a photographer and journalist."
